# Methodology — KESPA-NOTE-029

## Source basis

This technical note was prepared from the supplied September 24, 2026 KESPA engineering/Jira update describing the V2.3 lock-in release, autonomous-learning flywheel, scheduler/worker integration, evidence-gating rules, readiness audit, pause/audit expectations, and future growth gates.

No external source was used to upgrade implementation status or fill missing measurements.

## Inclusion rule

This release includes only facts that materially establish the **zero-user curriculum-driven learning checkpoint**:

- autonomous-learning persistence and curriculum,
- operating-system scheduling,
- asynchronous worker cadence,
- first recorded production run,
- research-request queue initiation,
- verification-age safeguard,
- benchmark-gap feeder boundary,
- publication and blind-promotion hard-off states,
- readiness-audit outcome,
- explicit limitations around downstream completion and controlled expansion.

## Deduplication boundary

This note is intentionally narrower than earlier KESPA public research artifacts.

It does not republish the earlier natural research-gap lifecycle as new work. Earlier work covered knowledge-gap detection during normal use, evidence acquisition/qualification, cross-host research handoff, Trusted Knowledge lifecycle mechanics, and freshness/reverification foundations.

The distinct contribution recorded here is **scheduled proactive research initiation with no active user**, an explicit curriculum, OS-level recurring execution, benchmark-gap inputs, and operational safety boundaries around what autonomous work may become.

## Verification interpretation

The note is marked `verified` because the source records concrete production validation of the subsystem's initiation path:

- daily-learning timer installed and enabled,
- first zero-user run completed,
- four research requests queued,
- last-run state confirmed by readiness audit,
- worker scheduling installed,
- hard-off publication/promotion safety state,
- readiness audit with zero failures.

The verified status is deliberately scoped. It does **not** mean every downstream research request was shown to complete source acquisition, claim extraction, verification, conflict handling, promotion, indexing, and future reverification.

## Planned-vs-operational handling

Statements phrased in the source as `should`, `future`, `next`, `expected`, or `planned` are retained as design requirements or limitations rather than converted into completed implementation claims.

Examples include the bulk curriculum importer, dedicated Learning Operations dashboard, some multi-level pause controls, large-corpus scaling work, general-learning privacy sanitization, and formal growth-checkpoint automation.

## Measurement integrity

No benchmark score was created for this technical note. Counts and timing values are retained only where directly recorded by the source. No inference was made from the four queued research requests to knowledge-card count, research success rate, verification rate, or downstream promotion rate.

## Publication privacy

The public package intentionally excludes credentials, private user data, private prompts/answers, exact private storage locations, raw internal logs/errors, authentication material, and other implementation-sensitive details that are not necessary to document the research architecture and verified checkpoint.
